# Getting Licensed

Every transmission on the amateur bands in Canada rests on one document: the Amateur Radio Operator Certificate. This topic covers how to earn it, what each qualification allows, and the rules in RBR-4 that every transmission has to follow. It's also section B-001 of the Basic exam, a quarter of the questions.

No prior knowledge is needed; the articles quote ISED's own documents and name the issue of each.
